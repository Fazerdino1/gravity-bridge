Gravity Bridge is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Many resources in this repository are **converted from third-party sources**. Each converted item keeps its own provenance and stays under its original license.

## Attribution policy

- Nothing is imported without an explicit license that allows redistribution.
- The copyright line below is **copied verbatim** from the source's license file, never reconstructed.
- Every converted file carries `source_url`, `source_path`, `source_commit`, `source_license` and, when known, `source_author` in its front matter.
- Items under a license other than MIT are marked in the *License* column and in their own metadata.
- Authors can ask for removal at any time (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#removal-requests)).
